📋 Strategy Rules (Winning Config)

📐 Timeframe
3-minute chart (NQ E-mini, $20/pt)
🕐 Session
NY Session only (9:30-11:30 AM ET / 13:30-15:30 UTC)
🔍 FVG Detection
Bullish: prev.high < next.low (gap up). Bearish: prev.low > next.high (gap down)
📏 Min FVG Size
2.0 points (smaller gaps filtered as noise)
⏳ FVG Maturity
Must wait 2+ bars after formation before retesting
✅ Retest Confirmation
Price must ENTER the FVG zone AND CLOSE above the zone MIDPOINT (bullish) or below midpoint (bearish)
🎯 Entry
At the close of the confirmation candle (immediate execution)
🛑 Stop Loss
Min(entry-2pts, zoneBottom-0.5pts) for longs. Max(entry+2pts, zoneTop+0.5pts) for shorts
🎯 Target
2:1 Risk:Reward minimum
🚶 Trail
Activates after price moves 1x risk distance. Locks in 50% of profits
💰 Risk Control
Min $40 risk, max $1,000 risk per trade. Skip trades where entry is too far from FVG zone
⏰ Session Close
All positions closed within last 3 minutes of NY session
